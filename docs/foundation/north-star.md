# North Star — The Ritual

> Owned by **vision**. The calibration for taste. This is the reference the
> Desirable gate is judged against on every Gate Card in this repo.

Keep it to one screen. If it's longer, the product isn't clear yet.

---

## The soul — one sentence

A founder's instrument that makes one model think like a disciplined founding
team — and refuses to ship anything unproven.

## The five-second promise

In the first five seconds, a founder should feel: *"this will make my product
better, and it will not bullshit me."* Sharpness and honesty, at once.

## What "insanely great" means here

- **Coherent by construction** — one law (four gates), one ladder, one artifact
  (the Gate Card) threading every movement. No loose parts.
- **It changes the decision, not the tone** — the method must alter the output, or
  it was cosplay. The whole self-test exists to prove this.
- **Evidence honesty is load-bearing** — never claim a rung you didn't reach;
  "owed to reality" beats an invented number, even in marketing.
- **Escape the mean** — it rejects the first obvious answer on purpose, with an
  Opposite and a 10× when the stakes justify it.
- **Runs anywhere** — pure instruction; it installs into whatever file a platform
  already auto-reads.

## The cliché we refuse

A "prompt pack" of personas and catchphrases — Steve-Jobs-says-think-different
theater that changes the voice and nothing else.

## The anti-vision

Cosplay over discipline. Disclaimers as a substitute for proof. A faked rung. A
yes-machine that can never say KILL. Any of these, even if it shipped and sold,
means we lost the soul.

## The one thing we will not trade

**Evidence honesty.** When forced to choose, we tell the truth about what we've
actually proven — and hand the rest to the founder — over sounding more certain
than we are.
