# North Star — Even

> A worked example: the founding taste-doc for the "Even" DEX concept, produced by
> running the Ritual on a cold brief. In a real product repo this lives at
> `docs/foundation/north-star.md` (the Ratchet hook reads it every session).

---

## The soul — one sentence

The first DEX that pays you back the money the bots were quietly taking — and
shows you, to the basis point, exactly how much.

## The five-second promise

A trader's first swap ends on a single line — *"+0.31% better than the best AMM,
funded by this block's winning bot bid of $4,200"* — and they feel, for the first
time in DeFi, *someone is on my side.*

## What "insanely great" means here

- Every trade surfaces the **edge returned**, in plain numbers, not buried.
- LPs see reclaimed LVR as its own **yield line** — "the money the bots used to
  take is now your income."
- **Zero slippage knobs.** One guaranteed price. The mechanism does the worrying.
- The auction is **legible** — you can see who bid and why your price improved.

## The cliché we refuse

"Another low-fee AMM / another cross-chain aggregator." Competing on surface
metrics (fees, chains, a prettier skin) while the structural wound — LPs bleeding
LVR to informed arbitrageurs — goes untouched. We fix the disease, not the symptom.

## The anti-vision

Becoming the new extractor: hiding the mechanism, or quietly keeping the
recaptured value as protocol margin. If we capture the bots' edge and don't return
it visibly, we have simply *become* the bot. That kills the soul even if it ships.

## The one thing we will not trade

**The recaptured value goes back to users and LPs, visibly.** When forced to
choose between revenue and that transparency, transparency wins — it is the entire
reason to trust Even over the incumbent.
